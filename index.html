01Since 2019

The valley answers back

Halvard Quarry is a bowl of granite forty metres deep. Shout into it and it shouts back a quarter-second later. In 2019 a handful of us carried a sound system down the old haul road to see what a kick drum would do in there. It did a lot.

Echo Fest is what that night grew into: 62 acts across four stages, built into the terraces of the quarry, lit from inside the rock and kept small on purpose. No VIP decks, no sponsor towers — just very good sound in a very strange place.

03Built into the terraces

Four stages, one rock

  1. 01

    The Bowl

    18:00 – 03:00

    The quarry floor. A 360° rig pointed straight up the walls, so the echo becomes part of the mix. Headliners after dark.

  2. 02

    Terrace

    14:00 – 00:00

    Cut into the second ledge with a view over the whole valley. Live bands, sunset sets and a very long bar.

  3. 03

    The Shaft

    22:00 – sunrise

    An old drainage tunnel, 120 metres of concrete and a sound system that will rearrange your ribs. Open 22:00 till sunrise.

  4. 04

    Moss Room

    11:00 – 23:00

    A quiet tent in the pines. Ambient, listening sessions, sound baths and the best coffee on site.

06Before you go

Plan your trip

Getting there

Trains run to Halvard station every 30 minutes. Our electric shuttle loops from the station to the gate from 12:00 till 04:00, free with every ticket.

Camping

The Pine Field opens Thursday at 16:00. Quiet zone after 01:00, family zone by the lake, lockers and charging in the barn.

Access

Step-free routes to every stage, viewing platforms at The Bowl and Terrace, a quiet room, and free carer tickets. Email us and we'll plan it with you.

Sound & safety

Free earplugs at every bar, sound levels kept under 100 dB at the front, welfare team on site around the clock.

07Good to know

Questions

Is there an age limit?+

18+ for the late stages after 22:00. Under-16s are welcome on Terrace and Moss Room until 21:00 with an adult.

Can I bring my own food?+

Yes. Food for your own use is fine anywhere; glass and camping stoves outside the Pine Field are not.

Is it cashless?+

Card and phone payments everywhere, and a cash top-up desk at the gate if you prefer.

What happens if it rains?+

The quarry drains fast and every stage is covered. Bring boots anyway — the haul road gets muddy.

Can I re-enter?+

Weekend holders can come and go freely. Day tickets allow re-entry until 23:00.

Is the tunnel on this page really listening?+

Only if you ask it to. The demo set is generated in your browser, and the microphone feed is analysed on your device and never recorded or sent anywhere.
